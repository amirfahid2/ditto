Ditto for iPhone and Android

Get Ditto.

Ditto is a board of big, tappable squares. Each square holds a piece of text you use again and again — a reschedule, a quick approval, a thank-you, the “let's find 15 minutes” you send ten times a day. Tap it and the text is ready to paste, or type it straight into any app with the Ditto keyboard.
