Ditto in a minute

So what is Ditto?
A board of big squares. Each one holds a piece of text you type again and again.
Like what?
Your address. Directions. A signature. The reply you send ten times a day.
And then?
Tap a square and it's copied, ready to paste.
Or open the Ditto keyboard inside any app and tap: the text is typed straight in.
Can it see what I write?
No. There's no account and no tracking. Your squares live on your phone.
