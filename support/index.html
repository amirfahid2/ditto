The keyboard

Your squares inside Messages, WhatsApp, Notes, Gmail, any app. Tap a square and its text is typed straight in.

How do I turn on the Ditto keyboard on iPhone?
  1. In Ditto, open Settings › Set up the Ditto keyboard and tap Open Ditto's settings.
  2. Tap Keyboards, then turn on Ditto.
  3. Allow Full Access appears under it. Leave it off. Ditto doesn't need it.
  4. In Messages, or any app, hold the 🌐 globe key at the bottom of the keyboard and pick Ditto.

Tap a square to type it. Tap the globe to go back to your regular keyboard.

How do I turn on the Ditto keyboard on Android?
  1. In Ditto, open Settings › Set up the Ditto keyboard and tap Open keyboard settings.
  2. Turn on Ditto in the list. Android calls it an on-screen or virtual keyboard.
  3. Android shows a standard notice for every keyboard that isn't Google's own. Tap OK. Ditto never sends what you type anywhere and keeps none of it.
  4. In any app, tap into a text field, then tap the keyboard icon in the bottom corner of the screen, or hold the space bar, and pick Ditto.

Tap a square to type it. Tap the globe to go back to your regular keyboard.

What is "Allow Full Access"? Should I turn it on?

No. Full Access lets a keyboard use the internet. Ditto never uses it: your squares are typed from a copy stored on your phone. Leave the switch off. You can check it any time in Settings › Ditto › Keyboards on your iPhone.

Why does my phone warn me about the keyboard?

Apple shows that notice for every keyboard that isn't Apple's, and Android shows one for every keyboard that isn't Google's, whether the keyboard needs anything or not. Ditto's keyboard only reads your own squares and only types when you tap one.

Can Ditto see my passwords or credit card numbers?

No. The Ditto keyboard keeps nothing you type. On iPhone it has no internet access at all. On Android it never sends what you type anywhere; it looks at the text field only to choose a blank line or a space before the next square, and to keep Select on the squares it just typed.

The only thing the keyboard counts is the squares you tap, for My stats, and that count stays on your phone.

How do I get my regular keyboard back?

Tap the globe. On Android you can also tap the keyboard icon in the corner of the screen.

Ditto can also hand back by itself once you stop tapping. In Ditto's Settings, Switch back to my regular keyboard offers Right after a tap, After a 4-second pause, After a 10-second pause or Only when I tap the globe. A pause lets you tap several squares in a row.

Do folders work in the keyboard?

Yes. Tap a folder in the Ditto keyboard and its texts drop open right under it, just like in the app. Tap it again to close it.

Your board

Squares as big as your thumb. Tap one to copy its text; press and hold to arrange.

How do I add a square?

Tap +. At the top, choose Text square or Folder. A text square copies its text with one tap; a folder holds more squares, so your board stays tidy.

Give it a name for the square and the text to copy. You can add a picture, an emoji or a photo, and pick a button color. The gray example notes in a new square are only hints: just type your own.

How do I move squares around, or put one in a folder?

Press and hold a square to arrange. Drag squares to move them, and drop one on a folder to put it inside. A square's menu also offers Move into a folder and Move out to the main board.

Can I copy several squares at once?

Turn on Combine quick taps in Settings. Tap another square within 5 seconds and its text is added to what's on your clipboard, separated by a blank line. Keep tapping to chain more squares.

Can a square fill in today's date or the time?

Yes. Write [TODAY], [TOMORROW] or [TIME] in a square's text and it becomes the real date or time when copied.

What are chat buttons and the Switch button?

The Switch button floats in the corner of the board and jumps straight to the app you pick, so you can copy and paste without hunting for it. Switch automatically after copy makes the jump right after a square copies.

Each chat button sits next to Switch and jumps straight into a Messages conversation. One person opens that chat; several people open your group text. You can have up to three.

Can I use Ditto in my language, or in dark mode?

Ditto and its keyboard come in 17 languages: English, 中文（简体）, हिन्दी, Español, العربية, Français, বাংলা, Português (Brasil), Русский, اردو, Bahasa Indonesia, Deutsch, 日本語, मराठी, Tiếng Việt, עברית and فارسی. Automatic follows your phone's language; you can also pick one in Settings.

Appearance in Settings offers Automatic, Light or Dark.

What is My stats?

Tap My stats under your board to see the squares you've tapped, the words they typed and the time that saved. Time saved is an estimate, based on how fast people type on a phone.

On iPhone, taps in the Ditto keyboard are counted in the keyboard: tap Stats at the bottom of the keyboard for your full total. On Android the app and keyboard are counted together. It's all counted on your phone and never sent anywhere.

Link to the web

Edit your board on a computer at dittotap.com. It's off until you turn it on.

How do I edit my board on a computer?
  1. On the computer, open dittotap.com/link.
  2. In Ditto, open Settings › Link to the web and tap Scan the code.
  3. Point the camera at the code on your computer.

Changes on your phone go to the web within a few seconds. Changes made on the web arrive on your phone the next time you open Ditto. Only link to a code you opened yourself: anyone with that code can see and change your board.

Is it private?

Your board is encrypted on your phone before it leaves, so the relay in between can't read it, and neither can we. Chat buttons and settings stay on your phone. The keyboard never goes online on iPhone, and on Android nothing you type with it is sent.

How do I stop it?

Open Settings › Link to the web and tap Unlink. The copy on the web is erased; your board stays on your phone. If no one edits for 30 days, the link ends by itself.

Ditto says my board changed in two places. Which do I keep?

It was edited on the web and on your phone since they last synced. Pick Use the web version or Keep this phone's; the other copy is replaced.

Ditto says it was unlinked, but I didn't unlink it.

The link was removed on the web, or it ended after 30 days without changes. Your board is still on your phone. To link again, open Settings › Link to the web.

Backups, sharing, and a new phone

Your squares live on your phone. Here is how to keep a copy, hand some to a coworker, or take them with you.

How do I back up my board?

Open Settings › Backup. Export board saves every square, folder and picture as a file you can keep in Files or send to yourself. On Android, Save to this device… saves the file where you choose.

Import board… brings a backup back, either added to your board or replacing it. Android also keeps Earlier versions… from before a replace.

How do I move Ditto to a new phone?

On the old phone, open Settings › Backup › Export board and send the file to yourself. On the new phone, install Ditto, open Settings › Backup › Import board… and choose that file. Pictures come with it.

On Android, the phone's own backup and transfer also carry your board when they are turned on. Link to the web starts unlinked on a new phone: scan the code again.

How do I share squares with a coworker?

Open Settings › Share board, choose the folders and squares, and tap Share link. Anyone with the link can tap the squares right away, even without Ditto. Pictures stay on your phone; choose Send as a file, with pictures to include them.

Got a board from someone? Open the link and Ditto asks whether to add it. Got a file? Open it in Messages, Mail or Files, tap Share, then choose Ditto.

Ditto Pro

Ditto is free to download and free for your first months. After that, Ditto Pro keeps everything working.

Is Ditto free?

Ditto is free for your first 4 months. After that, Ditto Pro is a yearly subscription through the App Store or Google Play that keeps every square, folder, picture and the keyboard working. The price is shown in Settings › Ditto Pro. People who were using Ditto on iPhone before version 1.4 kept a full free year.

What happens when the free months end?

Ditto asks you to subscribe before the board opens, and the keyboard shows a short note instead of your squares. Nothing is deleted: your squares, folders and pictures stay on your phone, and subscribing brings them right back.

How do I cancel?

iPhone
Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. The subscription renews yearly unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current year.

Android
Google Play › Subscriptions. Cancelling keeps Ditto Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

I subscribed, but Ditto says I haven't.

Open Settings › Ditto Pro and tap Restore purchases. Make sure the phone is signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with. If it still doesn't take, email us.

Can I get a refund?

Apple or Google handles the payment, so refunds go through them: on iPhone at reportaproblem.apple.com, on Android through your Google Play order history. We never see your payment details.

Privacy

We collect nothing.

What does Ditto collect about me?

Nothing. Ditto has no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your squares are stored only on your phone. If you turn on Link to the web, your board travels end-to-end encrypted, so we can't read it either. On Android, Google Play's billing library sends Google its own basic diagnostic reports about the billing service; we never receive them.

The privacy policy has the details.

The web version

Ditto in your browser, at dittotap.com.

Where are my squares kept in the browser?

In that browser, on that device. There is no account, so clearing the site's data, or using a private window, starts you with an empty board. In the web app's settings you can copy a backup as text and restore it there or on any other device.

Can I see my phone's squares in the browser?

Yes. Turn on Link to the web in the app and scan the code at dittotap.com/link. Your phone's board then shows in that browser, edits go both ways, and nothing we can read ever leaves your phone.
