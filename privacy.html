Ditto — Privacy Policy

Effective September 8, 2026

The short version

Ditto collects no data. Period.

What Ditto stores, and where

The text snippets you save in Ditto are stored only on your own device — in the app's private storage on iPhone, or in your browser's local storage on the web version. They are never uploaded, synced, or transmitted to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. Ditto has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The iPhone app makes no network requests.

Sharing

If you use "Share my list", the snippets you chose to share are encoded into the link itself and travel only through whatever channel you send that link with (for example, your own messages app). We never see them.
