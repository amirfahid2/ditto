Ditto for iPhone

Tap a square, paste anywhere.

Ditto is a board of big, tappable squares. Each square holds a piece of text you use again and again — an order set, a template, an address, a reply you type ten times a day. Tap it and the text is ready to paste, or type it straight into any app with the Ditto keyboard.

The Ditto keyboard

Folders inside folders

Group related texts. Tap a folder and it drops open right where it is.

Combine quick taps

In 17 languages

Choose Ditto's language inside the app, separate from your phone's.

We collect nothing.

Ditto collects no information whatsoever. Your squares live only on your phone. The app goes online only if you choose to link a web browser, and then your board is end-to-end encrypted, so we can't read it.

The keyboard works with “Full Access” left off and never uses the internet, so nothing you type can leave your device.

On Android, Google Play's billing library, which handles the subscription, sends Google its own basic diagnostic reports about the billing service. We never receive them, and none of your squares are included.
