Ditto Link to the web

Edit your board on a big screen.

  1. Open Ditto on your phone.
  2. Go to Settings (the gear at the top right) ▸ Link to the web.
  3. Scan this code.

Once your phone has scanned the code, you go back to dittotap.com with your phone's squares.

End-to-end encrypted. The code holds the key, and it goes only from this screen to your phone. Our relay only stores scrambled data it cannot read, and erases it 30 days after the last change. Privacy policy
