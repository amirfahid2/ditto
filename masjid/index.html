Masjid Guide

Today’s prayer times, read from your masjid’s own website.

Download on the App StoreAndroid: coming soon

  • Add a masjid by searching for it, or by pasting the link to the page on its website that lists the prayer times.
  • The times are read every morning, so today’s are already there when you open the app.
  • See how long until the next prayer. On Fridays, Jumu’ah takes Dhuhr’s place.
  • Reminders before each prayer at your home masjid: all five in one tap, or each on its own.
  • Widgets that count down to the next prayer on your Home Screen.
  • Five looks to choose from, and adjustable text size.
  • A Donate button that opens your masjid’s own donation page.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.
