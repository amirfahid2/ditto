Masjid Guide support

Adding a masjid

Swipe past your last masjid on the main page to the Add Masjid card, or open Settings (the gear, top right), then Masjids, then Add masjid. Search for your masjid by name (search is new and covers a few masjids so far), or paste the link to the page on the masjid’s website that shows the prayer times. If the times live on their own page, such as “Prayer Times”, use that link rather than the home page. The app reads the page and fills in the masjid’s name, which you can change. If no times are found, the app asks before adding it, and the masjid’s page offers “Change the link” and “Try again”.

The main page

Under the date you’ll see how long until the next prayer, for example “Asr in 1h 12m”. On Fridays, Jumu’ah takes Dhuhr’s place; on other days it is shown faded with “Fridays” under it. Tap “Refresh” at the bottom, or pull down, to read the masjid’s site again. Swipe sideways to move between masjids; the dots at the bottom show where you are, and the plus is the Add Masjid card.

Your home masjid

The house icon at the top left marks your home masjid. Alerts and widgets follow it. To change it, swipe to another masjid and tap the house, or in Settings, under Masjids, touch and hold a masjid and choose “Make home masjid”.

When the times are read

Each morning the app reads every masjid’s website in the background, so today’s times are already there when you open it. If the morning read didn’t happen, for example because the phone was off, the app reads the site when you open it.

“Haven’t been updated in at least 15 days”

If a masjid’s website has shown exactly the same times for 15 or more days, a small note appears at the bottom of that masjid’s page. Many masjids keep fixed iqamah times for weeks, so the note is only a reminder to check with the masjid. It disappears on its own as soon as the times change.

Alerts

The first time your home masjid’s times load, the app offers to remind you 15 minutes before each prayer with one tap. To change it later, open Settings, then Alerts. “All daily prayers” turns all five on or off, “Remind me” sets one time for all of them, and each prayer below can have its own time. Jumu’ah alerts come on Fridays only. When the website lists the adhan, “Count down to” lets reminders count down to the adhan instead of the prayer.

Notifications must be allowed for Masjid in your phone’s Settings app; the Alerts page has a button that takes you there if they’re off. Opening the app keeps the next twelve days of alerts scheduled. If you haven’t opened it for a while, a quiet note reminds you the day before they run out.

Widgets

iPhone. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and look for Masjid. There are three: a square countdown, a square with bigger numbers, and a wider one with all of today’s prayers. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap Lock Screen, then tap the space under the clock and pick Masjid. To give a widget a dark or light background, touch and hold it and tap Edit Widget.

Android. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Widgets, and look for Masjid. There are four: a square countdown, a square with bigger numbers, a strip, and a wide one with all of today’s prayers. Touch and hold a widget to give it a dark or light background.

On both, widgets use the look you picked in the app unless you choose a background for them.

Asking for the next prayer

iPhone. Say “Next prayer in Masjid” to Siri to hear the next prayer at your home masjid and how long until it. The same action is in the Shortcuts app.

Android. Touch and hold the Masjid icon on your Home Screen and tap “Next prayer”.

Reordering and removing masjids

Open Settings, then Masjids, then Edit. The main page follows the order you set. The app asks before removing a masjid, and tells you which masjid becomes home if you remove the home masjid.

iPhone. Drag masjids into a new order. Swipe left on a masjid, or touch and hold it, to remove it.

Android. Use the arrows to move a masjid up or down, and the bin to remove it. Touching and holding a masjid also offers Remove.

“Needs location” or “minutes after adhan”

Some masjids list a prayer as “x minutes after adhan” or “after sunset”. To turn that into a clock time the app needs the masjid’s location. The app tries to read the address printed on the website; otherwise tap the note on the masjid’s page, or open the masjid in Settings under Masjids, and type its city or address or tap “Use my current location”.

Looks, text size and the next prayer

Open Settings, then Appearance. Swipe through the pictures and tap the look you want. “Next prayer” shows a small preview of each way the coming prayer can stand out. Text size changes the size of the times on the main page, and they also grow with the text size set for your phone. Masjid works on iPad and Android tablets too.

Adhan times

When a masjid’s website lists the adhan as well as the iqama, “Show adhan times” in Settings, under Appearance, shows the adhan in smaller type under each prayer time.

Donate

If a masjid’s website has a Donate page, a Donate button appears at the bottom of that masjid’s page and opens the website in your browser. Donations happen on the masjid’s own site; the app never handles payments. To point the button at a different page, open the masjid in Settings under Masjids and fill in “Donate link”.
